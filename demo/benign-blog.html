Growing tomatoes on a city balcony

Cherry tomatoes are forgiving: six hours of sun, a deep pot and steady watering are enough for a summer of harvests.

Start seedlings indoors in early spring, harden them off for a week, then move them to 20-litre containers.

What worked

  • Self-watering pots
  • Weekly diluted seaweed feed
  • Pinching side shoots
