What PhishNetra does

Before each page loads, PhishNetra checks its address; after it loads, it checks the page's structure. Dangerous sites are blocked, suspicious ones get a warning, and password fields on unverified sites stay locked until you confirm.

Data disclosure — please read

To detect phishing, PhishNetra sends the following to the PhishNetra analysis server () for every web page you visit:

  • the page's full web address (URL)
  • the page title and up to 5,000 characters of its visible text
  • structural counts (number of links, forms, password fields, iframes, scripts, images)
  • the address of the page's icon (favicon)

It never reads what you type into forms, never sends passwords, cookies or browsing history, and the data is used only to compute a phishing verdict. Scan records may be shown on the PhishNetra demo dashboard. Full details: privacy policy.
